# Olist E-Commerce Data Analysis
Google Data Analytics capstone case study analyzing Brazilian e-commerce sales, delivery performance, and customer satisfaction using Python and Tableau.

## Business Task
Analyze Olist e-commerce data to identify sales patterns, customer satisfaction trends, and the relationship between delivery time and customer reviews.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- Tableau Public

## Key Findings
- São Paulo generated the highest sales at approximately R$5.20M.
- Health & Beauty was the highest-selling product category at approximately R$1.26M.
- The overall average customer review score was approximately 4.03.
- Orders delivered within 0–5 days had an average review score of approximately 4.35.
- Orders taking 31+ days had an average review score of approximately 2.17.
- Delivery time and review score showed a negative correlation of approximately -0.30.

## Recommendations
- Reduce delivery delays to improve customer satisfaction.
- Prioritize inventory and fulfillment capacity in São Paulo.
- Maintain strong inventory availability for high-selling Health & Beauty products.
- Investigate product quality, fulfillment, and customer complaints in lower-rated categories such as Office Furniture.
